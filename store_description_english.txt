Every movement matters. A single wrong unscrew can jam the plates or trap your bolts! Think ahead, plan your sequence, and watch the plates fall with satisfying realistic physics.

🔧 ADDICTIVE GAMEPLAY MECHANICS
Immerse yourself in an authentic workshop atmosphere. Tap to unscrew bolts and place them in available empty holes. Analyze how plates overlap, gravity's pull, and the swinging motion of free-hanging metals to find the perfect solution.
